# Tutorials

The `references/` files are depth, organised by topic and meant to be routed to. These are **walkthroughs**,
organised by when you need them, and grounded in one shoot that ran end to end: a two-camera seated interview for
a consumer-electronics partner, cut into a 14:50 programme, captioned, reviewed, published, and mined for vertical
social cuts.

Every number here was measured on that work or on the tests around it. Where something is unverified it says so.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Getting started — the mental model](01-getting-started.md) | An unmeasured number is worse than no number, because the operator cannot re-derive it on set. |
| 2 | [Prep: from "I have a shoot in three weeks" to a run sheet](02-prep-to-run-sheet.md) | Low chairs seat eye height 42–45", not the 47" your lighting plan assumed — and that moves every fixture. |

## Suggested order

**First time here:** read **1**, then skim **7**. Tutorial 1 is the contract; tutorial 7 is why the contract
exists, and it is the one that makes the rest feel necessary rather than fussy.

**Then follow the work.** 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 covers one shoot from "there is a shoot coming" to a delivered file,
in order. 8 and 9 are what happens afterwards, when a finished programme starts generating requests.

**Looking something up?** Use the triage table at the top of **7**, or the routing table in `SKILL.md`.

## What these are not

- Not a gear list. Every hardware number belongs to one specific body, card, mic or drive. See tutorial 1.
- Not a substitute for the references. Each tutorial points at the `references/` file that holds the detail —
  `method.md`, `onboarding.md`, `location-scout.md`, `qc-interpretation.md`, `offload-protocol.md`,
  `post-production.md`, `nle-backends.md`, `resolve-api.md`, `premiere-uxp.md`, `grade-match.md`.
- Not finished. The write-back protocol in tutorial 1 applies to these files too: when a shoot teaches something
  that would be true on the next one, it belongs in the durable layer, with a changelog entry — not left in a
  transcript.
